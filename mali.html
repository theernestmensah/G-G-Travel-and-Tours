Mali

About Mali

Mali is a landlocked country in West Africa, mostly in the Saharan and Sahelian regions. It is the eighth-largest country in Africa. Mali is bordered by Algeria to the north, Niger to the east, Burkina Faso and Côte d'Ivoire to the south, Guinea to the south-west, and Senegal and Mauritania to the west. The Niger River flows through its interior, providing a key source of water and transport. The capital is Bamako.
