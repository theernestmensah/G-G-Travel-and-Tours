Sierra Leone

About Sierra Leone

Sierra Leone is a country on the southwest coast of West Africa. It is bordered by Liberia to the southeast and Guinea to the north. Sierra Leone has a tropical climate, with a diverse environment ranging from savanna to rainforests. The country owes its name to the 15th-century Portuguese explorer Pedro de Sintra. It gained independence from the United Kingdom in 1961. The capital and largest city is Freetown.
