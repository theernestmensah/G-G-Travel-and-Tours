Togo

About Togo

Togo is a country in West Africa bordering the Bight of Benin. It is bordered by Ghana to the west, Benin to the east and Burkina Faso to the north. The country extends south to the Gulf of Guinea, on which the capital Lomé is located. A former German and then French colony, Togo gained independence in 1960. The economy rests largely on agriculture, although the country's extensive phosphate reserves are also significant.
