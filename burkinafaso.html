Burkina Faso

About Burkina Faso

Burkina Faso, formerly Upper Volta, is a landlocked country in West Africa. It lies south of the Sahara Desert. The country is bounded by Niger to the east, Mali to the north and west, and Benin, Togo, Ghana, and Ivory Coast to the south. A former French colony, it gained independence as Upper Volta in 1960. The name Burkina Faso, which means “Land of Incorruptible People,” was adopted in 1984. The capital is Ouagadougou.
