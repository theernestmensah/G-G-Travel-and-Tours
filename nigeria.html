Nigeria

About Nigeria

Nigeria is a country in West Africa, located on the Gulf of Guinea. It is the most populous black nation on Earth and is often referred to as the 'Giant of Africa'. It is bordered by Niger to the north, Chad to the northeast, Cameroon to the east, and Benin to the west. Nigeria is a federal republic comprising 36 states and the Federal Capital Territory, where the capital, Abuja, is located. The largest city is Lagos, one of the largest metropolitan areas in the world.
