Guinea-Bissau

About Guinea-Bissau

Guinea-Bissau is a tropical country on West Africa’s Atlantic coast that’s known for national parks and wildlife. It is bordered by Senegal to the north and Guinea to the south and east. The country has a history of political instability since gaining independence from Portugal in 1974. The official language is Portuguese, but a Portuguese-based creole is the national language. The capital is Bissau.
