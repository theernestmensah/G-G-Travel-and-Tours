Chad

About Chad

Chad is a landlocked country in north-central Africa. It is bordered by Libya to the north, Sudan to the east, the Central African Republic to the south, Cameroon and Nigeria to the southwest, and Niger to the west. Chad is a land of stunning landscapes, from the Sahara Desert in the north to the savannas in the south. The Ennedi Plateau, a UNESCO World Heritage site, is a vast sandstone massif with canyons, arches, and rock art. Zakouma National Park is one of the last strongholds for elephants in Central Africa.
