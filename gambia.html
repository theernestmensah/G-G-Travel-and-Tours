Gambia

About Gambia

The Gambia is the smallest country in mainland Africa, surrounded by Senegal, with a short coastline on the Atlantic Ocean in the west. It is a multiparty republic that gained independence from the UK in 1965. The country stretches for 450 km along the Gambia River, which is central to its geography and life. With a population of 2.5 million, it is one of the most densely populated countries in Africa.
